# Orbit Coffee Co. Leave Policy

This policy explains the time off available to all full-time employees of Orbit Coffee Co. Part-time employees receive the same benefits on a pro-rata basis.

## Annual leave

Every full-time employee receives 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave is earned at 2 days per month, so a person who joins in July has 12 days available for the rest of that year.

Unused annual leave can be carried over into the next year, up to a maximum of 5 days. Carried-over days must be used by 31 March, after which they expire. Leave is not paid out in cash except when you leave the company.

## Requesting time off

All leave is requested through Brewbook, our HR portal. Requests for more than three days should be submitted at least two weeks in advance. Your manager approves or declines the request within five working days.

## Sick leave

You get 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A doctor's note is only required if you are off sick for more than 3 consecutive days. Sick leave does not carry over to the next year.

If you fall ill during annual leave, tell your manager the same day and the affected days will be converted to sick leave.

## Parental leave

The primary caregiver of a new child, through birth or adoption, receives 26 weeks of fully paid parental leave. The secondary caregiver receives 6 weeks of fully paid leave, which can be taken any time within the first year.

## Sabbatical

After 5 years of continuous service, employees can take a 4-week paid sabbatical. It must be agreed with your manager at least three months ahead.

## Public holidays

Orbit follows the public holiday calendar of the office you are attached to. The full list for the year is published in Brewbook every December.
