# Life at Orbit Coffee Co.

How we work together, in and out of the office.

## Hybrid working

Orbit is a hybrid company. Teams come to the office on Tuesday and Thursday every week, so that collaborative days are shared. The rest of the week you can work from home or from the office, whichever you prefer.

Core hours are 11:00 to 16:00 in your local time zone. Outside core hours you can arrange your day as you like.

## Pets in the office

Dogs are welcome in the Bengaluru office on Fridays. Your dog must be registered with the office team first, be vaccinated, and stay with you on a lead in shared areas. Other pets are not allowed for allergy reasons.

## Learning and growth

Every employee has a learning budget of Rs 40,000 per year for courses, books and conferences. Unused budget does not roll over. Ask your manager before booking anything that costs more than Rs 10,000.

## Onboarding

New joiners are paired with a buddy for their first 90 days. Your buddy is a colleague from a different team who can answer the questions you don't want to ask your manager.

## All-hands meeting

The whole company meets at the all-hands on the first Monday of every month at 17:00. Leadership shares results, and anyone can ask questions anonymously beforehand.

## Coffee

Unlimited coffee, obviously. Each office has a tasting session for new roasts every Wednesday afternoon.
