# Orbit Coffee Co. Expenses and Travel

This document explains what Orbit pays for when you travel or spend money for work, and how you get reimbursed.

## Booking travel

All business trips are booked through TripNest, our travel booking platform. Booking outside TripNest is only allowed in an emergency and must be explained in the expense claim.

Flights shorter than 6 hours are booked in economy class. For flights longer than 6 hours you may book business class. Hotels must be within the nightly limit shown in TripNest for that city.

## Daily meal allowance

When travelling within India, meals are covered up to Rs 1,500 per day. For international travel the limit is USD 60 per day. Alcohol is never reimbursed, except as part of approved client entertainment.

## Mileage

If you use your own car for work, Orbit pays Rs 12 per kilometre. Your normal commute to the office does not count.

## Expense codes

Each claim needs an expense code. The most common ones are:

- EXP-10 for travel and transport
- EXP-21 for meals while travelling
- EXP-35 for software and subscriptions
- EXP-44 for client entertainment, which always needs written approval from your manager before the event

## Submitting a claim

Submit claims in Brewbook with a photo of every receipt. Claims must be submitted within 30 days of the purchase; later claims are rejected. Approved expenses are reimbursed with your next salary payment on the 28th of the month.

## Home office

New employees receive a one-time home office stipend of Rs 25,000 for a chair, desk or monitor. Keep the receipts, as they are needed for the claim.
