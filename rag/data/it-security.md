# Orbit Coffee Co. IT and Security Guidelines

Security is everyone's job. These rules protect our customers, our recipes and you.

## Passwords and sign-in

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. A passphrase of four or more random words is easier to remember than a complex short password. Never reuse your Orbit password on another website.

Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is required for every Orbit account. Use an authenticator app on your phone; SMS codes are not accepted.

## Working outside the office

Always connect to OrbitConnect, our company VPN, before working on public or shared Wi-Fi, such as in cafes, airports or hotels. Lock your screen whenever you step away from your device.

## Lost or stolen devices

If your laptop or phone is lost or stolen, report it to the security team within 1 hour by calling the IT helpdesk on extension 4040. The team can lock and wipe the device remotely. You will not be blamed for reporting a loss, but a late report puts company data at risk.

## Software and hardware

Install software only from the Orbit Store, our self-service app catalogue. If the tool you need is missing, request it through the IT helpdesk. USB storage drives are blocked on all company laptops; use the shared drive to move files.

Company laptops are replaced every 3 years.

## Phishing

Suspicious emails should be reported with the "Report phishing" button in your mail app. Do not click links or open attachments in a message you were not expecting. The security team runs a practice phishing test every quarter.
